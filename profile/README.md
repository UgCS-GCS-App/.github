**So, what is UgCS, exactly?**
UgCS is a ground control station application for Windows that lets drone operators plan, simulate, and fly autonomous missions from a single desktop workspace. Beyond simple waypoint flights, it's built to handle mapping and survey routes, terrain-aware elevation, and payload setup for the cameras and sensors carried on the mission — which is what most people mean when they talk about serious UgCS drone mission planning rather than just remote-control flying.

### Is UgCS right for you?
**Do you fly more than one type of drone?** Likely yes — UgCS supports a broad range of MAVLink-compatible autopilots, and DJI aircraft are covered separately through UgCS for DJI, so mixed fleets can be planned from the same project. **Do you need repeatable survey routes?** UgCS builds grid and corridor patterns for mapping work rather than requiring you to fly them manually. **Do you want to check a mission before committing to it?** Routes can be reviewed and simulated against the terrain profile first.

### Okay, it's installed — now what?
1. The first thing most people do is create a new mission project and pick the drone or drones they'll be flying.
2. From there, a route gets drawn directly on the map — either a simple waypoint path or a survey grid over the area of interest.
3. Before anything leaves the ground, the plan gets checked in simulation, with elevation and payload settings reviewed alongside it.
